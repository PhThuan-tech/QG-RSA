\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{EFCIL with pre-trained models.}
Prompt- and adapter-based methods freeze a pre-trained backbone and learn a
small number of parameters per task or shared across
tasks~\cite{wang2022l2p,zhou2024ease}. Training only the adapter shrinks
forgetting at the representation level. The classifier then becomes the
weak point, because its old rows are never trained against new classes.
SLCA~\cite{zhang2023slca} introduced classifier alignment with Gaussian
pseudo-features. RSIAT~\cite{rsiat} adds a representation-steering loss on
the base task and drift compensation of the stored means.

\paragraph{Using class statistics directly.}
Several methods classify with the stored statistics themselves rather than
re-training a classifier on samples drawn from them.
RanPAC~\cite{mcdonnell2023ranpac} solves a ridge regression in a random
feature space. FeCAM~\cite{goswami2023fecam} uses per-class Mahalanobis
distances. Drift-compensation methods~\cite{yu2020sdc,goswami2024adc,gomezvilla2024ldc}
and EFC~\cite{magistri2024efc} focus on keeping the statistics valid.
Our fusion is complementary to these works: it keeps the CA classifier and
adds a generative score, and the oracle experiments
(Section~\ref{sec:gap}) show that better statistics alone help little.

\paragraph{Quantum kernels and measurements.}
Quantum feature maps define kernels through state
overlaps~\cite{havlicek2019supervised,schuld2019feature}. Projected quantum kernels
compare reduced density matrices to mitigate the exponential concentration of
fidelities~\cite{huang2021power,thanasilp2024concentration}. The pretty-good
(square-root) measurement~\cite{hausladen1994pgm} discriminates a set of
mixed states. It is within a square of the optimal success
probability~\cite{barnum2002reversing} and coincides with the least-squares
measurement~\cite{eldar2001srm}. That last property is the key to our
negative result for the PGM.

\paragraph{Variance in benchmarks.}
Training randomness alone can produce differences comparable to published
improvements~\cite{bouthillier2021accounting}. Our protocol follows this line
of work and adapts it to incremental learning.
